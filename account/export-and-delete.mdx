## Get a copy of your journal

An export button isn't available in the app yet. Email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) from the address you signed up with and ask for a copy of your entries, insights and follow-ups. An [AI assistant you connect](/guides/connect-ai-assistant) can also read your entries.

## Delete entries or memories

Deleting single entries or memories in the app isn't available yet. Email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) and say what you want removed.

## Delete your account

Email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. This removes your account, journal, insights, follow-ups and memories, and can't be undone.
