Your journal is private. This page explains what dazie keeps and why.

## What dazie stores

- Your account details (email, name, onboarding answers)
- Conversation transcripts and the journal entries written from them
- Insights, follow-ups and memories derived from your entries
- Your companion settings

## Where it is stored

Account and journal data are stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Conversations are processed by AI providers to transcribe speech, run the conversation and write your entry.

## What dazie never does

- Sell your data or share it with advertisers
- Use your journal entries to train AI models
- Send journal content to analytics tools

## The public website

The marketing site at www.dazie.io has its own [privacy notice](https://www.dazie.io/privacy.html), covering hosting and optional analytics.
