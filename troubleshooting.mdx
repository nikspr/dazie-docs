## My companion doesn't answer

Reload the dashboard and send your message again. If it still doesn't answer, email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) with the time it happened.

## I don't see journal entries

Journal entries are written from scheduled calls, which are [coming soon](/guides/scheduled-calls), or added by an [AI assistant you connect](/guides/connect-ai-assistant). Chat conversations in the web app don't create entries yet.

## I can't sign in

- Check your inbox and spam folder for the confirmation email, and confirm your address first.
- If you've lost your password, email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) from the address you signed up with.

## My AI assistant can't connect

The first connection can time out while dazie's server wakes up. Try again after a minute. See [Connect your AI assistant](/guides/connect-ai-assistant).
