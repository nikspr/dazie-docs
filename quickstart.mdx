<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.dazie.io](https://app.dazie.io) and create an account. Sign up with your email and confirm it from the link in your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell dazie about yourself">
    Onboarding asks a few short questions: what you'd like to get out of journaling, what's going on in your life right now, and how you'd like to be addressed. Your answers give your companion context, so it doesn't start from zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape your companion">
    Choose its name and describe the companion you want, for example *"supportive, curious, helps me explore creative projects"*. See [Your companion](/guides/your-companion).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start talking">
    Open your dashboard and write to your companion in the chat. It answers and asks follow-up questions. Scheduled phone check-ins, which turn a conversation into a journal entry, are [coming soon](/guides/scheduled-calls).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for the first week

- Pick one moment of the day for reflecting and keep it. Evenings work for most people.
- Don't prepare. Say whatever is on your mind first; the follow-up questions do the rest.
- Name specifics. "The meeting with Sam" gives your companion something to remember; "work stuff" doesn't.

Read [Building a journaling habit](/guides/building-a-habit) for more.
