---
title: "Follow-ups"
description: "dazie captures the to-dos and intentions you mention while voice journaling and brings them back in later conversations."
---

When you say something like *"I need to call my sister this week"* or *"tomorrow I'm going to start before checking email"*, dazie captures it as a follow-up.

## Where follow-ups appear

- Attached to the entry they came from
- In a combined list under **Your journal → Follow-ups**

## How they come back

In a later conversation dazie may ask how a follow-up went. You can mark it done, push it, or drop it, out loud or in the app.

Follow-ups are deliberately lightweight. dazie is not a task manager, and it won't send reminders outside your conversations.
