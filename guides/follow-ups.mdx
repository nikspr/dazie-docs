When you say something like *"I need to call my sister this week"* or *"tomorrow I'm going to start before checking email"*, dazie captures it as a follow-up.

## Where follow-ups appear

- Attached to the entry they came from
- In a combined list under **Your journal → Follow-ups**

## How they come back

In a later conversation dazie may ask how a follow-up went. An [AI assistant you connect](/guides/connect-ai-assistant) can list your follow-ups and mark them done or dropped.

## Reminders and calendar events

Reminders and calendar events for time-bound follow-ups are planned for a later release. Today follow-ups are a list, not alerts.

Follow-ups are still deliberately lightweight. dazie is not a task manager.
