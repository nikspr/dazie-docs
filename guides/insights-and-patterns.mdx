After each conversation dazie analyses the entry and adds insights to it. Over time these add up to patterns across entries.

## Per-entry insights

Each entry can include:

- **Key themes**: the two or three topics the conversation was really about.
- **Mood and energy**: a short read on how you sounded, in words rather than a score.
- **A reflection**: one sentence that names something you might not have noticed, for example *"Your reflections show a pattern of seeking balance between work and rest."*

## Patterns over time

A **Patterns** view that shows what keeps coming back across entries (topics, people, recurring frustrations, changes in mood across weeks) is planned. Today each entry carries its own insights, and an [AI assistant you connect](/guides/connect-ai-assistant) can search your journal for recurring themes.

## Using insights well

Insights are prompts for reflection, not verdicts. If one feels wrong, say so in your next conversation. dazie takes corrections into account.
