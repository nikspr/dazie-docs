<Note>Scheduled calls are coming soon. They are not available yet in early access.</Note>

With scheduled calls, dazie will call your phone at a time you choose, for example a morning check-in or a wind-down call in the evening. You answer and talk as you would with a friend who asks good questions. When the call ends, dazie writes up the conversation as a journal entry with insights and follow-ups.

## Why a call

The hardest part of journaling is starting. A call removes the decision: the phone rings, you pick up, and the conversation has already begun.

## What you can do today

Create your companion at [app.dazie.io](https://app.dazie.io) and talk with it in the web app. Your companion, your goals and what it learns about you carry over once calls are available.
