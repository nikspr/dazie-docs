<Note>Scheduled calls are available to every account since Lantern, dazie's November 2026 release.</Note>

With scheduled calls, dazie calls your phone at a time you choose, for example a morning check-in or a wind-down call in the evening. You answer and talk as you would with a friend who asks good questions. When the call ends, dazie writes up the conversation as a journal entry with insights and follow-ups.

## Why a call

The hardest part of journaling is starting. A call removes the decision: the phone rings, you pick up, and the conversation has already begun.

## Set up your calls

During onboarding at [app.dazie.io](https://app.dazie.io), the step **when should we connect?** asks how often dazie should call (daily, every few days, weekly or every two weeks) and at what time. The next step, **where can I reach you?**, asks for your phone number.

## Plans

The Explore plan is free and includes 3 phone check-ins a week. Flourish costs $9 a month for unlimited check-ins. Between calls, you can talk with your companion in the web app's chat.
