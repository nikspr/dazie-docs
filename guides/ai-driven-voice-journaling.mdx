Voice journaling means reflecting out loud instead of on paper. An AI companion turns that conversation into a journal entry, asks follow-up questions, and remembers what you share over time.

## How voice journaling supports reflection

- **Less friction.** Speaking is usually faster than typing, which makes a daily entry easier to start. See [Voice journaling](/guides/voice-journaling).
- **Questions that go deeper.** dazie follows one thread of a conversation and asks the next question. See [Follow-ups](/guides/follow-ups).
- **Memory across conversations.** dazie keeps track of the people, goals and events you mention, so later conversations can build on earlier ones. See [Memory](/guides/memory).
