Your companion is who you talk to in dazie. You shape it during onboarding.

## Name, personality and purpose

During onboarding you give your companion a name, pick personality traits such as *supportive*, *curious* or *structured*, and describe what you want from journaling, for example *"help me explore creative projects"* or *"keep me honest about my goals"*. dazie uses this to set tone and how hard it pushes on follow-up questions.

## Focus areas

Onboarding also asks what you want to focus on, such as work, health, a creative project or a relationship. Your companion steers toward these when a conversation has no clear direction.

## Changing your mind

Editing your companion after onboarding isn't available in the app yet. Email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) if you want it changed.
