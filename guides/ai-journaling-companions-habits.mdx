A journaling habit usually breaks at the start: remembering to open the journal and deciding what to write. A companion that calls you at a set time removes that step, and memory makes each conversation pick up where the last one ended.

## The call is the cue

Habits form faster when they're tied to the same time and place. With dazie you choose how often your companion calls (daily, every few days, weekly or every two weeks) and at what time, during onboarding. The phone rings, you pick up, and the conversation has already begun. See [Scheduled calls](/guides/scheduled-calls) and [Building a habit](/guides/building-a-habit).

## A companion that remembers

dazie keeps track of the people, projects, goals and events you mention and brings relevant ones into later conversations, for example asking how the interview you mentioned last week went. See [Memory](/guides/memory).

## Follow-ups that come back

When you say you'll do something, dazie captures it as a follow-up, attached to the entry and collected under **Your journal → Follow-ups**. In a later conversation it may ask how it went. Reminders and calendar events are planned for a later release; today follow-ups are a list, not alerts. See [Follow-ups](/guides/follow-ups).

## Make it yours

You choose your companion's name, personality, purpose and the focus areas you want to reflect on, such as work, health or a relationship. See [Your companion](/guides/your-companion).

## Plans

Scheduled phone check-ins are available to every account since Lantern, dazie's November 2026 release. Explore is free and includes 3 phone check-ins a week, which is enough for a steady habit. Flourish costs $9 a month for unlimited check-ins.

## Privacy

Your journal is stored in the EU (Frankfurt), never sold and never used to train AI models. dazie is a reflection tool, not therapy. See [Data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy).
