---
title: "Journaling prompts"
description: "Voice journaling prompts for daily reflection, stress, gratitude, goals and relationships that you can use with dazie or on your own."
---

dazie picks its own opening question, but you can start a conversation with any prompt. Say it at the start: *"Today I want to talk about…"*.

## Daily reflection

- What took most of my energy today, and was it worth it?
- What's one moment from today I'd want to remember in a year?
- What did I avoid today?

## Stress and overwhelm

- What's actually on my plate right now? Say all of it out loud.
- Which of these worries can I do something about this week?
- What would "good enough" look like for the thing I'm stressed about?

## Gratitude

- Who made today easier, and do they know?
- What's something small that went right?

## Goals and growth

- What did I say I'd do last week, and did I do it?
- Where am I stuck, and what's the smallest next step?
- What am I better at than I was three months ago?

## Relationships

- Which conversation from this week am I still replaying?
- Who have I not talked to in too long?

<Tip>
dazie remembers what you talked about. A good follow-up prompt is simply: *"What did I say I'd work on last time?"*
</Tip>
