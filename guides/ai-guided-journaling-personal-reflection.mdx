AI-guided journaling means a companion asks questions during the entry instead of leaving you with a blank page. Each question builds on what you just said.

## How a guided conversation works

A reflective conversation moves through three phases:

1. **Check-in.** You say what is on your mind.
2. **Going deeper.** Your companion follows one thread with a follow-up question.
3. **Wrapping up.** You land on a takeaway or next step.

See [Voice journaling](/guides/voice-journaling) for more on this structure.
