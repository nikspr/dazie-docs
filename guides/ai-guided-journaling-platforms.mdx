## How dazie handles follow-ups and memory

dazie is a journaling companion you set up during onboarding. You choose its name, personality, purpose, and focus areas, then talk with it by text in the web app.

- **Follow-up questions:** during a conversation, dazie asks follow-up questions about what you shared.
- **Companion memory:** dazie remembers what you share over time and uses it in later conversations.
- **Journal view:** your entries are available in the web app.
- **Connected assistants:** you can connect your own AI assistant over MCP to read your journal and add entries, to-dos, and memories.
- **Follow-ups:** to-dos and intentions you mention are kept as a list in your journal. [LINK TO FOLLOW-UPS GUIDE].
