dazie is built to remember. It keeps track of the people, projects, goals and events you mention, so it can ask *"remember when you mentioned feeling stuck? Let's explore how you've grown since then."*

## What gets remembered

- People you talk about and your relationship to them
- Ongoing projects, goals and decisions
- Events coming up, such as a trip or an interview
- Preferences you state about how you like to journal

## How memory is used

At the start of a conversation dazie looks at what's relevant from memory and uses it to ask better questions. It won't bring up something you asked it to drop.

## Viewing and removing memories

A page for viewing and deleting memories in the app is not available yet. An [AI assistant you connect](/guides/connect-ai-assistant) can list what your companion remembers. To have memories removed, email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io).
