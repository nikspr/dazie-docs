Voice journaling means reflecting out loud instead of on paper. You speak, and the record of what you said becomes your journal.

## Why speak instead of write

- **Less friction.** Most people speak three to four times faster than they type, and nobody edits themselves mid-sentence while talking.
- **More honest.** Spoken reflection tends to be looser and less polished, which is usually where the useful material is.
- **A conversation, not a page.** With dazie, someone asks the next question. That's the part a blank journal can't do.

## Anatomy of a good conversation

A good reflective conversation runs 5 to 15 minutes and moves through three phases:

1. **Check-in.** You say what's on your mind. *"How was your day?"*
2. **Going deeper.** Your companion follows one thread. *"What part of that is still bothering you?"*
3. **Wrapping up.** You land on a takeaway or next step. *"Is there anything you want to do differently tomorrow?"*

## Getting more out of each entry

- **Name specifics.** "The meeting with Sam" gives dazie something to remember; "work stuff" doesn't.
- **Say how you feel, not only what happened.** Insights are built from both.
- **Steer when you want to.** Say "let's talk about something else" at any point.
- **End deliberately.** Saying "that's it for today" lets dazie close the conversation cleanly and write the entry.

## Where your entries live

Journal entries appear in **Your journal** on your dashboard, newest first, with their insights and follow-ups attached. Entries are written from scheduled calls, which are [coming soon](/guides/scheduled-calls), or added by an [AI assistant you connect](/guides/connect-ai-assistant). Today you talk with your companion in the web app's chat.

## Frequently asked: how are my entries stored and accessed?

Your account and journal are stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Only you can see them when you sign in at [app.dazie.io](https://app.dazie.io), and an AI assistant can read them only if you [connect one](/guides/connect-ai-assistant) and approve it. Your journal is never sold or used to train AI models. To get a copy or have it deleted, see [Export and delete](/account/export-and-delete).
