A journaling habit depends on starting again the next day. AI journaling companions can lower the effort of starting and give each conversation something to build on. This guide explains how that works and what dazie offers today.

## How dazie supports a regular habit

- **Follow-up questions:** during a conversation, dazie asks about what you shared, so you do not need to plan every entry. [LINK TO FOLLOW-UPS GUIDE].
- **Companion memory:** dazie remembers what you share, so a new conversation can pick up from earlier ones.
- **Text in the web app:** you talk with your companion by text in the web app.
- **Connected assistants:** you can connect your own AI assistant over MCP to read your journal and add entries and to-dos. [LINK TO CONNECT AI ASSISTANT GUIDE].

Not available yet:

- **Scheduled phone check-in calls:** coming soon.
- **Reminders and calendar events:** planned for a later release. Follow-ups are a list today, not alerts.
