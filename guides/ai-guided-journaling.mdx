AI-guided journaling tools ask questions about what you just wrote or said, and some keep context for later conversations. This guide explains what that adds and what to compare when you choose one.

## What AI guidance adds

A blank page asks you to start from nothing. A guided journal asks a follow-up question about what you just shared, which helps you go one level deeper instead of stopping at what happened.

## What to compare

- **Follow-up questions:** whether the tool asks about your own entry or rotates fixed prompts.
- **Memory:** whether it keeps context from earlier conversations, and how you can see or remove it.
- **Input:** whether you type, speak, or both, and whether the tool starts the session or waits for you.
- **Access to your journal:** whether you can read, export and delete your entries.
- **Pattern analysis:** whether trends across entries are available today or only planned.
- **Pricing:** what is free and what costs money.

## How dazie handles it

- **It starts the conversation:** your companion calls you at a time you choose for a [scheduled phone check-in](/guides/scheduled-calls). You can also talk with it by text in the web app.
- **Follow-up questions:** it asks about what you shared and follows the thread you pick.
- **Memory:** it remembers the people, projects, goals and events you mention. A page for viewing and deleting memories is not available yet; email [niklas@dazie.io](mailto:niklas@dazie.io) to have memories removed. See [Memory](/guides/memory).
- **Insights:** each entry carries key themes, mood and energy, and a one-sentence reflection. A Patterns view across entries is planned. See [Insights and patterns](/guides/insights-and-patterns).
- **Follow-ups:** to-dos and intentions you mention are collected under **Your journal → Follow-ups**. See [Follow-ups](/guides/follow-ups).
- **Your own assistant:** Claude or any MCP client can read your journal and add entries after you approve it. See [Connect an AI assistant](/guides/connect-ai-assistant).
- **Pricing:** since Lantern, dazie's November 2026 release, scheduled phone check-ins are available to every account. Explore is free with 3 check-ins a week; Flourish is $9 a month for unlimited check-ins.

## Privacy

Account and journal data are stored in the EU (Frankfurt) and processed by AI providers only to run your conversations and write your entries. Your journal is never sold, never shared with advertisers and never used to train AI models. For a copy or deletion, see [Export and delete](/account/export-and-delete).

## Choosing a tool

Start with the problem you want solved. If you keep forgetting to journal, look for a tool that starts the session for you. If you want depth, compare follow-up questions and memory first. If you want a history you can trust, compare export and privacy terms.
