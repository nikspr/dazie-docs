Some AI journaling tools only store what you write. Others ask questions in the conversation and keep track of what you share. This guide explains how AI-guided reflection works and where Dazie fits.

## How AI-guided reflection works

Dazie is a voice-first AI journaling companion. You create a companion during onboarding, then talk with it by text in the web app. During a conversation, it asks follow-up questions and remembers what you share, so later conversations can build on earlier ones.

Dazie is a reflection tool, not therapy.
