An AI journaling companion is an app that asks you questions about your day, remembers what you share, and keeps a journal of your conversations. dazie is one of these companions. It is in early access and free during early access.

## How a companion supports a habit

- **Short daily conversations.** A conversation can be a few minutes. On a busy day, you can keep it to one or two questions.
- **Follow-up questions.** dazie asks follow-up questions during a conversation, so each entry goes one level deeper than a blank page.
- **Long-term memory.** dazie remembers the people, projects, goals and events you mention, and uses them to ask better questions. See [Memory](/guides/memory).
- **Follow-ups from conversations.** To-dos and intentions you mention are captured as follow-ups and listed under **Your journal**. See [Follow-ups](/guides/follow-ups).

## What is available today

- Conversations with your companion in the web app's chat.
- A journal view with your entries, newest first.
- Connecting your own AI assistant over MCP, so it can read your journal and add entries, to-dos and memories. See [Connect an AI assistant](/guides/connect-ai-assistant).

## Data handling

Your account and journal are stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Your journal is not sold or used to train AI models. To get a copy or have your data deleted, see [Export and delete](/account/export-and-delete).
