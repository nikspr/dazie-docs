Voice journaling lets you reflect out loud instead of typing. An AI companion adds the part a blank page can't: someone asks the next question, remembers what you said last time, and writes the entry for you.

## What voice-first journaling means

With dazie you talk instead of type. Your companion calls you at a time you choose for a [scheduled phone check-in](/guides/scheduled-calls), asks what's on your mind and follows the thread you pick. When the call ends, dazie writes the conversation up as a journal entry. Between calls you can also talk with your companion by text in the web app.

Speaking is usually faster and looser than writing, and nobody edits themselves mid-sentence. That looser material is often where the useful reflection is. See [Voice journaling](/guides/voice-journaling) for how a good conversation flows.

## Follow-up questions

During a conversation your companion asks follow-up questions about what you just shared, and asks one more when an answer is short. A typical conversation runs 5 to 15 minutes: a check-in, going deeper on one thread, and wrapping up with a takeaway.

## Long-term memory

dazie keeps track of the people, projects, goals and events you mention. At the start of a conversation it looks at what's relevant and uses it to ask better questions, and it won't bring up something you asked it to drop. See [Memory](/guides/memory).

## What each entry contains

Each entry can carry key themes, a short read on your mood and energy, and a one-sentence reflection, plus the follow-ups you mentioned. A **Patterns** view across entries is planned and not available yet. See [Insights and patterns](/guides/insights-and-patterns).

## Plans

Scheduled phone check-ins are available to every account since Lantern, dazie's November 2026 release. The Explore plan is free and includes 3 phone check-ins a week. Flourish costs $9 a month for unlimited check-ins.

## Connect your own AI assistant

Claude or any MCP client can read your journal and add entries, to-dos and memories after you approve it. See [Connect an AI assistant](/guides/connect-ai-assistant).

## Privacy

Account and journal data are stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Your journal is never sold, never shared with advertisers and never used to train AI models. dazie is a reflection tool, not therapy. See [Data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy).
