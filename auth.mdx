---
title: "Authentication for agents"
description: "What needs registration on dazie and what does not."
---

## Reading the docs

No registration is needed. The pages, their Markdown twins and the MCP server at `https://docs.dazie.io/mcp` are public and take no authentication.

## Acting for a user

dazie has no public API and no OAuth flow, so agents cannot register for an account or act on a user's behalf. A person registers at [app.dazie.io](https://app.dazie.io/) with an email address and password and confirms it by email.
